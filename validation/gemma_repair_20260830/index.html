    MMAU holdout detail:

    • The E4B and 12B final sets had no prior Gemini 2.5 Pro outputs. Their IDs had appeared in earlier Gemini 3.7 Flash experiments, so these are specialist output holdouts, not globally unseen question-ID holdouts.
    • Within each formal run, candidate selection used EXPLORE and the reported e-value used the untouched 150-case CONFIRM split.
    • A stricter globally unseen-ID run was also attempted first. The Flash gates were positive but underpowered (E4B 8/1, 12B 10/4) and are not successes.
