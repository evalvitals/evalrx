CONCLUSION: The failures are not a decoding/scaffolding artifact (truncated generations, bad extraction, or sampling noise) but a genuine audio-perception capability gap: on missed items the model falls back to a positional/letter prior instead of grounding its choice in the clip, and it does so with the same one-token confidence it shows on items it gets right, so it "guesses fluently" rather than visibly struggling.

EVIDENCE_CHAIN:
- coverage_verification_gap shows a near-perfect FAIL association (effect=-1.0, p=0.000155) driven by an all-or-nothing pattern per case (n_correct is either 0/5 or 5/5, coverage_gap_rate=0.0, no_coverage_rate=0.5) — half the sampled items are never answered correctly across 5 resamples, which is a deterministic capability ceiling, not a selection/voting defect that a verifier could fix.
- format_sensitivity corroborates this from an independent angle: positional_bias and format_flip_rate are both strongly higher on FAIL cases (effect +0.44 and +0.51, both surviving BH), meaning on questions it misses, the model's chosen letter shifts when the option order is rotated — a letter-prior signature (reinforced by the skewed modal_letter_histogram: D=19, C=14 vs A=6, B=9) rather than content-tracking.
- calibration confirms the model doesn't know it's guessing: conf_logprob is significantly lower on FAIL than PASS (effect -0.34, p=0.00018) but still averages high in absolute terms, producing a large overconfidence_gap (0.336 logprob-channel ECE, 0.466 verbal-channel gap) — consistent with confident commitment to a prior-driven answer rather than calibrated uncertainty.
- termination_audit's "truncation" signals (output_chars, looks_truncated, recovered_by_continuation) do correlate with FAIL, but per the analyzer's own confound-control caveat this is a red herring since 100% of cases (clean_rate=0.0) share the same 1-3-character, tag-less answer format regardless of correctness — the model always emits a bare letter with no rationale, so these length-based signals just re-detect "was the case a genuine miss" rather than revealing a distinct truncation failure mode.

QUALITATIVE:
- The model never produces a chain-of-thought or scoreable rationale (selfcheck_consistency skipped all 32 cases for "no scoreable sentence"), so there is no reasoning trace to audit for hallucination — it goes straight from audio+prompt to a single letter.
- self_consistency at n=5 shows perfect raw and semantic agreement (consistency=1.0, 1 unique cluster) even on effectively-temperature-1 sampling, meaning the model is not stochastically uncertain when wrong — it commits to the same (often letter-biased) wrong answer every time, which is a stronger indictment of true audio-grounding failure than a noisy-decoding one.
